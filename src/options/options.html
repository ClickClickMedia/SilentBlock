SilentBlock

 

Protection

Turns every filter on or off, everywhere.

Changes apply to pages as they load. Reload open tabs to see them.

Paused sites

SilentBlock does nothing on these sites or their subdomains. Pausing is also the fix when a site breaks.

    Nag-wall sites

    On these sites SilentBlock watches for "turn off your ad blocker" walls and removes them as the page loads. Add one from the popup with "Always on this site".

      Colour the icon by page status

      Blue: nothing to block. Green: ads and trackers blocked. Amber: a pushy site, pop-ups or nag walls stopped. Red: malware or scam hosts blocked. Grey: paused. Off keeps the icon blue.

      Show count on icon

      Show the number of blocked requests on the toolbar icon.

      Backup

      Settings live only in this browser. Export them to move to another browser or keep a copy.

      Filter lists

      Lists are bundled with each release and never downloaded at runtime.
